# Design Brief: ifratelli accesorios

## 1. Creative Direction & Vibe
*   **Aesthetic:** Mediterranean, artisanal, warm, and effortless.
*   **Vibe:** Classic and timeless, but highly approachable. It should feel like stepping into a sunlit, elegant local artisan market.
*   **UI Philosophy:** The interface should remain uncluttered and breathable. The design must frame and elevate the product photography, not compete with it. 

## 2. Color Inspiration
*   **Base:** Avoid stark, clinical whites or pure blacks. Lean toward soft, organic neutrals (like natural linen or raw cotton) for backgrounds.
*   **Palette:** Earth tones inspired by the materials used in the jewelry displays—warm wood browns, stone greys, and muted botanical greens.
*   **Accents:** Warm metal tones, specifically gold, to highlight key elements or subtle interactions.

## 3. Typography Mood
*   **Headers:** Should evoke the 1998 heritage of the brand. Look for elegant, classic serif choices that feel established but not overly formal.
*   **Body Copy:** Clean, minimal, and highly readable.
*   *(Note to frontend-design skill: Please select and pair specific fonts that achieve this balance between heritage and modern readability.)*

## 4. Custom Assets & Iconography
*   **Primary Rule:** The UI must prioritize the custom, hand-drawn SVGs located in `data/visual/`. These were exclusively created by the brand's Lead Designer to capture the handmade essence of *ifratelli*.
*   **Implementation:** Use these custom assets for navigation, social links, the shopping cart, user profiles, and general UI icons. Do not default to rigid, generic icon libraries unless a critical utility icon is missing. 

## 5. Shape & Texture
*   **Forms:** Avoid overly sharp, aggressive corners. Favor subtle, organic rounding on cards and images that mimics the natural shapes of the jewelry (cowrie shells, beads).
*   **Depth:** Use very soft, diffused shadows to mimic natural sunlight rather than harsh, artificial drop shadows.
